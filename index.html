Electromechanical weighing station image coming soon
Project 05 2024

Electromechanical Weighing Station & PID Control

Designed and built an electromechanical weighing station capable of measuring loads from 0 to 1 kg with a 10 g target resolution. The system integrated a load cell, spring-assisted mechanical range extension, NI USB-6001 data acquisition, rotary position feedback, and a DC motor driving an analog indicator. A LabVIEW-based closed-loop controller used signal filtering, sensor calibration, and PID control to reduce oscillation and position the dial accurately, with measured weight deviations maintained within approximately ±8 g.
