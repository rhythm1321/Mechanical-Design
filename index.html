Composite Wall Heat Transfer Analysis

Analyzed steady-state heat transfer through a multi-material composite wall using analytical thermal-resistance methods and COMSOL Multiphysics. Numerical simulations were validated against analytical results and used to investigate fixed temperature, convection, and applied heat-flux boundary conditions. A mesh sensitivity study was also performed to verify convergence and simulation accuracy.
